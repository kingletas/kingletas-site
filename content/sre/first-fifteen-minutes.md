The first fifteen minutes decide most incidents. Name one person in charge, stop the damage before explaining it, say what you know and nothing more, and write everything down as it happens.

Work down the page while it's happening. Tick each step as it's done and the time goes beside it; fill in who holds which role and what you've learned, and copy a summary into your team's chat whenever someone asks where things stand.
